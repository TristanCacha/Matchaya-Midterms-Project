# Matchaya image credits and verification

All product and brand photographs were generated with OpenAI image generation on 10 October 2026 for this IT0049 sample project. They are fictional product depictions, not photos of food served by a real café. No third-party brand, person, or stock photograph was used. The [OpenAI Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/) state that, as between the user and OpenAI and to the extent permitted by applicable law, the user owns the output. The output may not be unique. This is the applicable use basis; there is no stock-photo license or attribution requirement.

The 17 product images were visually reviewed against the corresponding product-specific accuracy rules. The contact sheet is `docs/image-contact-sheet.jpg`. Each product has a 640×640 WebP and a 1280×1280 WebP in `public/assets/demo/products/`; the public set totals 1.80 MiB, and each 640 px tile is below 80 KiB. Hero and About images are in `public/assets/brand/`. Source WebP files and the one-step rebuild script are included so an image can be replaced by updating its `source_file` in `database/seeds/image-manifest.json` and running `python tools/build_demo_images.py`.
